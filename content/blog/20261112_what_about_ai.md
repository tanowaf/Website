The plan is to optimize development for the long-term, not immediate returns.

Which, as of today, means:

Phase 1: write the first implementation fully by hand, in order to get deeply acquainted with all the ins and outs of the
subject matter of http proxies and firewalls

Phase 2: eventually rewrite the application in Rust for performance and safety. That will most likely happen with a substantial
dose of AI support
